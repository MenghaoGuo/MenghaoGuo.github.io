About Me

I am a "Shuimu Scholar" postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University, working with Prof. Shi-Min Hu. I received my Ph.D. from the same department, also under the supervision of Prof. Shi-Min Hu, and my B.Eng. from Xidian University.

I have published papers in top venues such as T-PAMI, ACM TOG, SIGGRAPH, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR and CVPR, including two CVMJ Best Paper Awards. I have received the Tsinghua University Special Scholarship (the highest honor for students at Tsinghua) and the National Scholarship. I am also a core developer of the Jittor deep learning framework. My research is supported by the Postdoctoral Innovative Talent Support Program (博新计划), Class A, and was previously supported by the inaugural NSFC Young Students Basic Research Program (Ph.D. Students). I was also recognized among the World's Top 2% Scientists by Stanford University.
